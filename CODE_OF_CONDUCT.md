# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Tugboat is built for people doing difficult work, including people who may be under sustained stress. We want participation here to be respectful, useful, and safe for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, disability, or health status.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with patience and respect.
- Critique ideas and behavior without attacking the person.
- Make room for different goals, constraints, and lived experiences.
- Protect privacy. Do not pressure anyone to disclose diagnoses, treatment, private conversations, or identifying project details.
- Accept correction, repair harm where possible, and keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, personal attacks, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not accepted.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository activity and to anyone representing Tugboat in other community spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive details publicly. To report conduct concerns, first use a private contact method made available by a maintainer. If none is available, open an issue containing only a request for private contact. A maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action after reviewing the available information.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
